Positioning Lab Privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Positioning Lab is a student study resource operated by Connor Kelly. Google sign-in is optional. You can study without creating an account or connecting Google.

Information saved on your device

The site saves your study progress, learned marks, bookmarks, preferences, profile name, badges, and personal questions in your browser. If you use cloud sync, your browser also keeps a sync baseline and a recovery copy from before your most recent import.

Clearing your browser’s site data removes these local copies. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

Optional Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, Google provides your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information. Your Google account identifier connects you to the correct cloud save.

Positioning Lab does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, Google Drive files, Calendar, or contacts. Google sign-in tokens are held temporarily in page memory while you are connected. They are not saved in browser storage or the study database.

What cloud sync stores

If you connect your account, the site saves your study history, correct and incorrect answer counts, learned marks, bookmarks, badges, preferences, profile name, and personal questions to a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. Personal questions may include images you attach or embed.

This information lets you restore and synchronize your study progress across devices. XP, ranks, and category progress are calculated from your saved study data.

Who can access the information

The cloud storage Sheet is managed by the site owner and is not publicly shared. The site owner can access the information stored in it. Cloud saves are not end-to-end encrypted.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google provides sign-in, Apps Script, and cloud storage for this feature. These providers may process technical information, such as network requests and IP addresses, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can use the site without signing in, export your progress, back up your personal questions, or sign out at any time. Signing out stops cloud syncing but does not delete your existing cloud save or the copy stored on your device.

Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see its local study data. Use a separate browser profile on shared devices.

Keeping and deleting data

Local data stays in your browser until it is removed or replaced. Cloud data is retained until the site owner deletes it, including in response to a verified deletion request. Private backup copies may remain until those backups are replaced or removed.

To request deletion of your cloud save, contact the site owner. You may be asked to verify the associated account before deletion. Deleting a cloud save does not automatically delete copies on your devices or backups you downloaded.

Personal questions and images

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, identifiable patient images, or other sensitive personal information. Only upload material you are allowed to use and store.
